Setup Guide

  1. In Google Forms, create a new form for each of the seven sections.
  2. Add the questions exactly as listed in the tables above.
  3. In the Responses tab, click the Sheets icon and choose "Select existing spreadsheet".
  4. Pick your VF Gift Shop Google Sheet, then choose the matching tab (Products, Portfolio, Testimonials, Why Us, How to Order, Sales Reps, or Payouts).
  5. Make sure each form question title matches the existing column header in your sheet so submissions land in the right column.
  6. Copy each form's share link and paste it into the FORM_LINKS block at the bottom of admin.html.

About column names: the site reads fields like image_url, display_order, is_visible, is_active, rep_id, commission_rate, and order_amount. Name your form questions to match the headers already in your sheet (including any underscores) so they map correctly. When in doubt, open the sheet and copy the exact header text.

Tip: If a response form creates a new sheet with its own headers, you can also link it directly and the site will still work — just make sure the header names match. To keep everything in one place, use "Select existing spreadsheet" and point at the correct tab.
