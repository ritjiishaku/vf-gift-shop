Setup Guide

  1. In Google Forms, create a new form for each of the three sections.
  2. Add the questions exactly as listed in the tables above.
  3. In the Responses tab, click the Sheets icon and choose "Select existing spreadsheet".
  4. Pick your VF Gift Shop Google Sheet, then choose the matching tab (Products, Portfolio, or Testimonials).
  5. Make sure the response columns line up with your existing headers. The site reads these columns: name, description, image_url, category, price, price_from, icon, display_order, is_visible (Products); image_url, caption, display_order, is_visible (Portfolio); quote, name, rating, source, display_order, is_visible (Testimonials).
  6. Copy each form's share link and paste it into the FORM_LINKS block at the bottom of admin.html.

Tip: If a response form creates a new sheet with its own headers, you can also link it directly and the site will still work — just make sure the header names match. To keep everything in one place, use "Select existing spreadsheet" and point at the correct tab.
